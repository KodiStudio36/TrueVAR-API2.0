Poomsae Timing

Poomsae has no PSS vests or plexi helmets to track — courts are balanced by total running time. One estimate covers every match, whether it's a solo cut-off performance or a head-to-head duel (both take about the same time on the floor).

One system applies to every category; exceptions override it. "Any" matches everything in that column. Cut-off automatically adds a Preliminary Round (20+ entrants) and Semi-Final (9+) — pick "Final only" to skip them.
