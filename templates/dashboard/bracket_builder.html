Brackets & Court Ordering

Everything below runs in your browser — nothing is written to Firestore until you hit Push to Firebase. Configure courts and vest numbers (these save immediately, like the category manager), then Generate to compute brackets and court ordering as a local draft you can drag-and-drop to adjust before committing. Three hard locks are enforced and re-checked before every push: the rest gap between the same athlete's matches, no same-club first-round pairs when avoidable, and one court per category.

{# ── CONFIG ROW: courts (local only) + vest sizes (auto-derived) ────── #}

Courts

Pre-filled from this tournament's court count. This is session-only — nothing here is saved to Firestore, so it resets if you leave the page. Vest numbers can repeat across courts (different physical vests). The min-gap number is a hard lock: two matches that can involve the same athlete are always at least that many fights apart (the court holds if it has to). The ID field must exactly match that court's own TrueVAR-Core court_id — a mismatch here is why a court-side server would see no matches at all, with no error to point at it.

Vest Size & Hit-Level

Derived automatically by weight from whichever PSS hit-level table is selected below — not assigned per tournament. Switching tables updates this preview immediately.

Equipment

Session-only, like Courts. Vest counts are keyed by size + generation — the scheduler tries not to double-book more vests of a size/gen than you enter here, accounting for a transfer buffer when a vest is reused on a different court. Plexi helmets work the same way: the "Plexi" checkbox on a court above is a soft preference (categories needing one incline toward that court), not a hard requirement — actual availability is governed by the helmet count below plus the same transfer buffer. Equipment is soft: if nothing is free after a short wait, the match is placed anyway and flagged.

Poomsae Timing

Poomsae has no PSS vests or plexi helmets to track — court assignment here is just about keeping every court's total running time roughly even. One estimate covers every match, whether it's a solo cut-off performance or a head-to-head duel (both take about the same time on the floor).
