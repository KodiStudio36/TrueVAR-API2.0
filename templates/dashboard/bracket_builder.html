Brackets & Court Ordering

Everything below runs in your browser — nothing is written to Firestore until you hit Push to Firebase. Configure courts and vest numbers (these save immediately, like the category manager), then Generate to compute brackets and court ordering as a local draft you can drag-and-drop to adjust before committing.

Courts

Pre-filled from this tournament's court count. This is session-only — nothing here is saved to Firestore, so it resets if you leave the page. Vest numbers can repeat across courts (different physical vests); the rest-gap applies globally across every court running in parallel. The ID field must exactly match that court's own TrueVAR-Core court_id — a mismatch here is why a court-side server would see no matches at all, with no error to point at it.

Vest Size & Hit-Level

Derived automatically by weight from whichever PSS hit-level table is selected below — not assigned per tournament. Switching tables updates this preview immediately.
